# M6.2 entry contract

M6.1’s deterministic substrate may be trusted: strict protocol/capsule/record validation, exact fingerprints, M4 admission adapter, evidence enrichment, projection drift detection, and bounded correction are proven.

M6.2 introduces only the first production SkillProtocol: TDD. Qualification must prove its method, not merely final correctness. Deterministic fixtures precede one retained governed live qualification. TDD uses exactly `repository.read`, `repository.edit`, `test.execute`, and `implementation.change`, with edit authority only.

No other production skill begins. M6.2 must not redesign the substrate unless TDD qualification exposes a genuine general defect.
