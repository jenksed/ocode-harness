# M6.1 entry contract

## Immutable assumptions

M0–M5 remain proven input evidence. M6.1 starts from the M6.0 recovery SHA and trusts M4’s sole AdmissionRequest/AdmissionDecision engine, capability vocabulary, authority/permission/subject reconciliation, M5 EvidenceRef v1 freshness and evidence ladder, normalized OpenCode `model_output`, and existing execution ledger.

## M6.0 decisions M6.1 may trust

- Canonical skill authority is the committed pair `skills/<id>/protocol.json` and `skills/<id>/SKILL.md`; qualification records are immutable source at `skills/<id>/qualifications/<fingerprint>.json`.
- `.opencode/skills/<id>/SKILL.md` is a generated, ignored runtime projection only. Source/projection drift must be deterministically detected before use.
- Skill requirements map exactly to M4 `AdmissionRequest`; `compatible_skills` is legacy advisory metadata and roles never grant eligibility.
- Fingerprinting uses the exact UTF-8, RFC 8785/NFC, domain-separated algorithm in [M6 contracts v1](m6-contracts-v1.md). A changed fingerprint makes previous qualification `STALE`.
- Capsules, qualification lifecycle, two-phase evidence integrity, bounded context expansion telemetry, initial live-qualification policy, and one-correction structured-output policy are exactly defined in [M6 contracts v1](m6-contracts-v1.md).

## Permitted M6.1 implementation

M6.1 may add focused implementation/tests/docs for protocol validation and canonicalization; semantic fingerprint calculation; lifecycle/QualificationRecord validation and derivation; deterministic source-to-projection rendering and drift checking; ContextCapsule/EvidenceCapsule validators; requirements-to-AdmissionRequest adapter; and a bounded skill-domain parser that consumes existing normalized `model_output`.

It may add the first Ocode-native protocols and their canonical SKILL.md files only when their fixture/qualification scope is explicitly approved by the M6.1 slice. It may add generated-file ignore rules and focused operator-free fixtures. It must reuse existing M4/M5 modules rather than copy their schemas.

## Prohibited redesign

Do not alter M4 vocabulary, admission, authority, permission projection, reason codes, subject reconciliation, agent registry, provider/model routing, OpenCode transport normalization, EvidenceRef freshness, ledger ownership, or install/update/rollback semantics without a separately approved milestone. Do not authorize via role name or `compatible_skills`; do not implement Planner Compiler, selection, scheduling, Runner, worktrees, parallel execution, memory, or external skill installation.

## Focused proof

M6.1 proof should demonstrate closed-schema rejection; the required fingerprint canonicalization vectors; invalidation from either canonical file; qualification state derivation including A→B `STALE`; exact M4 request mapping and deny behavior; projection generation/drift rejection; ReportedEvidenceCapsule-to-EvidenceCapsule enrichment with no model-minted authoritative facts or dangling evidence IDs; ContextCapsule bounds and justified bounded expansion telemetry; initial-skill live qualification required before `QUALIFIED`; and normalized-output parsing with at most one correction. It need not reconstruct or rerun M0–M5 acceptance/live evidence.
